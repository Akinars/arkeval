Social Science Research & Evaluation

Dr. Alana Kinarsky helps foundations, nonprofits, and faith-based organizations answer a hard question: is this program actually working. She designs and leads evaluations — mixed-methods studies, policy analysis, implementation research — that give funders and boards evidence they can act on.

She works as a research data analyst at UCLA's School of Education while running ARK Evaluation, her independent consultancy founded in 2015.

Evaluation Policy

Evaluation policies shape how organizations understand, plan, and use evaluation. They are essential for managing evaluations effectively at scale.

The Evaluation Policy Research Hub collects resources to help practitioners and scholars understand existing evaluation policies and write new ones.

Research & Publications

My academic research looks at how evaluation actually happens in practice. That includes analyzing evaluation policy across the philanthropic sector, mapping how US foundations spend on evaluation, and studying the federal evaluation market. I also write on evaluation theory, and earlier research looked closely at the experience of foster youth.

Below is a selection of my published research. For a full list, visit my Google Scholar page.
